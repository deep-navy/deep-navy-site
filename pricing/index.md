<section class="content-section"><div class="shell"><div class="section-heading centered-heading"><span class="eyebrow">Pricing questions</span><h2>Clear before you activate.</h2></div><div class="faq"><details><summary>What is an Engineering Credit?</summary><p>A customer-facing unit that abstracts underlying model, compute, storage, and service cost. It allows plan limits and usage reporting without asking customers to reason about raw tokens.</p></details><details><summary>Can usage exceed the included balance?</summary><p>Only under the plan and controls agreed during onboarding. The platform is designed to show balance and usage before additional charges are incurred.</p></details><details><summary>Why is early-access pricing scoped?</summary><p>The initial objective, repositories, runtime needs, and support level materially affect a responsible pilot. We agree on the subscription and credit balance before checkout; the site does not fabricate a universal price.</p></details><details><summary>Does checkout happen on this site?</summary><p>The signed-in application requests a short-lived Checkout Session from the Deep Navy API and redirects to Stripe. If billing is not configured, it reports that dependency as unavailable and does not simulate a purchase.</p></details></div></div></section>
