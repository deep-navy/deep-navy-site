<section class="section workflow-section" aria-labelledby="workflow-title">
  <div class="shell split-heading">
    <div><span class="eyebrow">From intent to evidence</span><h2 id="workflow-title">A work loop you can inspect.</h2></div>
    <p>Agents do not disappear behind a prompt. You can follow the objective through plans, issues, pull requests, approvals, spend, and measured outcomes.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="shell workflow-steps">
    <article><span>01</span><div><h3>Define the outcome</h3><p>Give the team a business objective. Deep Navy proposes KPIs and an initiative plan for your review.</p></div></article>
    <article><span>02</span><div><h3>Connect the work</h3><p>Install the GitHub App and select only the repositories this team should access.</p></div></article>
    <article><span>03</span><div><h3>Watch the team deliver</h3><p>See sessions, agent-to-agent messages, tool use, diffs, issues, and draft pull requests as they happen.</p></div></article>
    <article><span>04</span><div><h3>Approve and measure</h3><p>Keep sensitive actions behind explicit gates and connect shipping activity back to usage, cost, and product outcomes.</p></div></article>
  </div>
</section>
