<section class="content-section">
  <div class="shell content-grid">
    <header><span class="eyebrow">The operating loop</span><h2>From objective to measured result.</h2></header>
    <ol class="timeline">
      <li><div><h3>State the business objective</h3><p>Describe the user or business result you want—not a preselected implementation. The team converts it into a structured objective.</p></div></li>
      <li><div><h3>Review KPIs and initiatives</h3><p>The product roles propose how success should be measured and which pieces of work are most likely to move it. You stay in the decision loop.</p></div></li>
      <li><div><h3>Let specialists coordinate</h3><p>Design, product, backend, reliability, engineering, and review roles work through one shared plan and exchange explicit handoffs.</p></div></li>
      <li><div><h3>Inspect the work in progress</h3><p>Follow sessions, A2A messages, tool activity, issues, diffs, checks, pull requests, and spend without asking for a status recap.</p></div></li>
      <li><div><h3>Approve protected actions</h3><p>Actions covered by your policy pause for an authorized human. An approval is a recorded decision, not a chat message.</p></div></li>
      <li><div><h3>Measure delivery and outcome</h3><p>Compare the cost and pace of the initiative with the KPI signal it was created to improve.</p></div></li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</section>
