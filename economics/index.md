<section class="content-section">

    <header><span class="eyebrow">Engineering Credits</span><h2>A stable unit for plans and guardrails.</h2></header>
    <div class="prose"><p>Plans include Engineering Credits: a customer-facing billing abstraction over underlying model, compute, storage, and service cost. The provider-level detail remains available to Deep Navy for reconciliation, while customers get a comprehensible shared balance.</p><div class="feature-cards"><article class="feature-card"><span class="number">01</span><h3>Included balance</h3><p>A plan includes a defined credit pool shared by eligible engineering teams.</p></article><article class="feature-card"><span class="number">02</span><h3>Visible usage</h3><p>Customers can see balance and attributed consumption before surprises become invoices.</p></article><article class="feature-card"><span class="number">03</span><h3>Usage controls</h3><p>Team and plan limits are designed to enforce the commercial and operational guardrails.</p></article><article class="feature-card"><span class="number">04</span><h3>Reconciliation</h3><p>Provider records and the durable cost ledger support retry-safe, auditable accounting.</p></article></div></div>

<section class="content-section">
  <div class="shell content-grid"><header><span class="eyebrow">Honest interpretation</span><h2>Attribution is not causation.</h2></header><div class="prose"><p>Deep Navy can attach an initiative to a KPI and show the timing of engineering work and observed outcome signals. It should not claim a change caused an outcome without an appropriate experimental design.</p><div class="callout"><strong>Evidence over theater</strong><p>Dashboards distinguish cost records, delivery facts, observed product signals, and inferred relationships. The system does not expose hidden model reasoning as proof.</p></div></div></div>
