<section class="page-hero"><div class="shell"><span class="eyebrow">Engineering economics / customer record</span><h1>Usage, cost, and outcome evidence share a timeline.</h1><p>Deep Navy reconciles provider usage into a durable ledger, attributes cost to the team and initiative, and reports credits against the active plan balance.</p><div class="page-meta"><span>Field reference / v0.1</span><span>Updated July 17, 2026</span></div></div></section>

<section class="content-section" aria-labelledby="ledger-title"><div class="shell content-grid"><header><span class="eyebrow">Ledger / 01</span><h2 id="ledger-title">Provider event to customer record.</h2></header><ol class="timeline"><li><div><h3>Collect usage</h3><p>Model, compute, storage, and service events arrive with a provider identifier and observation time.</p></div></li><li><div><h3>Reconcile</h3><p>Retry-safe ingestion associates the provider record with one durable ledger entry.</p></div></li><li><div><h3>Attribute</h3><p>The ledger associates the entry with customer, team, role, objective, initiative, and delivery artifact when those references are present.</p></div></li><li><div><h3>Report</h3><p>EconomicsService returns attributable cost, credits used, credits remaining, and the measurement timestamp for the selected scope.</p></div></li></ol></div></section>

<section class="content-section" aria-labelledby="fields-title"><div class="shell content-grid"><header><span class="eyebrow">Customer fields / 02</span><h2 id="fields-title">A small, measured surface.</h2></header><div class="data-table-wrap"><table class="data-table"><thead><tr><th>Field</th><th>Meaning</th><th>Decision supported</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Attributable cost</td><td>Reconciled provider cost associated with the selected team or initiative</td><td>Compare work scopes on a consistent currency basis</td></tr><tr><td>Credits used</td><td>Customer credit consumption recorded against the active plan</td><td>Track consumption during the billing period</td></tr><tr><td>Credits remaining</td><td>Measured plan balance after recorded usage</td><td>Review capacity before further work</td></tr><tr><td>Measured at</td><td>Timestamp of the returned summary</td><td>Assess freshness before making a decision</td></tr></tbody></table></div></div></section>

<section class="content-section" aria-labelledby="scope-title"><div class="shell content-grid"><header><span class="eyebrow">Attribution / 03</span><h2 id="scope-title">Cost follows the work hierarchy.</h2></header><div class="data-table-wrap"><table class="data-table"><thead><tr><th>Scope</th><th>Recorded reference</th><th>Review question</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Customer / team</td><td>Organization and team identifiers</td><td>Where is plan capacity being consumed?</td></tr><tr><td>Role</td><td>Canonical runtime role</td><td>Which responsibility incurred the usage?</td></tr><tr><td>Objective / initiative</td><td>Product work identifiers</td><td>What did this work scope cost?</td></tr><tr><td>Delivery artifact</td><td>Issue, commit, check, or pull request reference</td><td>Which recorded change accounts for the work?</td></tr><tr><td>Outcome event</td><td>KPI series, source, value, and observation time</td><td>What signal was observed after delivery?</td></tr></tbody></table></div></div></section>

<section class="content-section" aria-labelledby="interpretation-title"><div class="shell content-grid"><header><span class="eyebrow">Interpretation / 04</span><h2 id="interpretation-title">Read timing with the measurement design.</h2></header><div class="prose"><p>Attribution shows where usage accrued and which product work it accompanied.
