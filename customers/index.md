<section class="page-hero"><div class="shell"><span class="eyebrow">Founding customers</span><h1>Build the operating model with us.</h1><p>Deep Navy is in early access. We are working with a small number of teams that value measurable outcomes, reviewable work, and candid product feedback.</p><div class="page-meta"><span>No fabricated customer logos</span><span>No invented case studies</span></div></div></section>

<section class="content-section"><div class="shell content-grid"><header><span class="eyebrow">A strong fit</span><h2>Teams ready for a focused pilot.</h2></header><div class="feature-cards"><article class="feature-card"><span class="number">01</span><h3>A real objective</h3><p>You have a measurable product or business outcome and can identify a bounded first initiative.</p></article><article class="feature-card"><span class="number">02</span><h3>An active repository</h3><p>Your team uses GitHub and can grant repository-scoped access to a dedicated GitHub App.</p></article><article class="feature-card"><span class="number">03</span><h3>A human owner</h3><p>Someone can review proposed KPIs, answer product questions, and make protected decisions.</p></article><article class="feature-card"><span class="number">04</span><h3>An evidence mindset</h3><p>You care about the quality, cost, and product effect of work—not just generated code volume.</p></article></div></div></section>

<section class="content-section"><div class="shell content-grid"><header><span class="eyebrow">The pilot</span><h2>Start narrow. Earn wider scope.</h2></header><ol class="timeline"><li><div><h3>Readiness conversation</h3><p>We confirm the objective, repository, access boundary, and what “working” means for your team.</p></div></li><li><div><h3>Secure onboarding</h3><p>You create an account, install the GitHub App, select repositories, and activate the agreed plan.</p></div></li><li><div><h3>One objective</h3><p>The engineering team proposes KPIs and an initiative. You review the plan before protected work proceeds.</p></div></li><li><div><h3>Evidence review</h3><p>We review delivery, quality, cost, and the observed outcome signal together before expanding scope.</p></div></li></ol></div></section>
